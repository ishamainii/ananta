I simulate galaxies, and I turn old Indian mathematics into things you can walk around inside.

Ananta (अनन्त, "without end") is a series of interactive chambers. Each one takes a result from Indian mathematics, writes the math as tested code, and renders it as light and sound. Every claim links to the paper it comes from.

    Research

    emory university · jul 2025 – now

    astrophysics · simulation-based inference

    Forward-modeling IllustrisTNG galaxies into mock MaNGA observations

    I turn simulated galaxies into synthetic integral-field spectroscopy that looks like what the MaNGA survey measures, then run it through GIST, the same analysis pipeline used on real data. Along the way I profiled the pipeline, found why it couldn't scale, and built an LLM assistant that reads our real outputs.

    About

    I'm Isha, a student at Emory working at the intersection of astrophysics, data science and visual math. This project started from a question: what would Pingala's binary meters or Mādhava's infinite series look like if you could stand inside them?
